Anchor Backgammon privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Erol Ertan develops Anchor Backgammon. You can play without creating an account. Your matches and statistics stay on your device. The free game uses Google AdMob to show ads, and Google Play handles purchases.

Games and settings on your device

Anchor Backgammon stores your games, move history, reviews, statistics, settings and unlocked features on your device. It uses a locally generated player ID to associate your game records with your profile. The developer does not receive these records or the player ID.

Android keeps these records in the app’s private storage. To erase them, clear Anchor Backgammon’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. This clears your local progress. A Premium purchase remains associated with the Google Play account that bought it and can be restored after reinstalling.

Ads in the free game

Anchor Backgammon requests non-personalized ads. When the game requests or displays an ad, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share the following data for ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention:

  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location, such as your city.
  • App and device information, including the app and operating system versions, device identifiers such as the app set ID, and performance information such as load times.
  • Interactions with the app and its ads, such as ad impressions, taps and video views.

The app disables access to the Android advertising ID. Google encrypts the ad data it transmits using TLS. Google handles and retains data received by its advertising services under its own policies. How Google uses information from partner apps · How Google retains data

Where consent is required, the game uses Google’s consent form before requesting ads. You can revisit available choices in Settings → Ads and privacy. Your choices can limit which ads Google serves. An ad request still sends your IP address to Google.

Premium and purchases

Google Play processes payment for the one-time Premium upgrade. The game checks purchase status with Google Play, acknowledges completed purchases and stores the unlock status on your device. The game and its developer do not receive your payment card details. When Premium is active, the game skips ad requests. Google manages purchase records according to its own policies. Children

Anchor Backgammon is intended for players aged 13 and older.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions, email Erol Ertan at eertan.dev@gmail.com. If you email, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include so that you can receive a response. Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date.
